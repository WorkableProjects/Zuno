THE DDS CHEVRON
Unbounded draws a "<" chevron into the left side of B and E. Dutch Display Sans makes that
chevron the signature of the whole typeface:
  - Capitals: the chevron is drawn into the upright left stroke of every capital that has one
    at mid-height, matched per weight to the original B (depth ~57-60 units, apex at y=385).
    Stems and round letters (O, C, G, Q, D, U...) take the full chevron; diagonal legs
    (A, V, W, X, Y, K arms) take a half-depth facet so the stroke reads as cut, not broken.
    S and Z keep their original form: their left side at mid-height is a diagonal spine, and
    forcing the chevron there distorts the letter.
  - Lowercase: the same chevron, scaled to the x-height (72% depth, apex at half x-height),
    applied to every lowercase letter with an upright left stroke. Ascenders and descenders
    are left straight, so the signature sits in the x-height band.
  - Figures and symbols: 0-9 and & ? ! $ # % @ follow the capital rule.
  - Weight behaviour: on heavy weights the chevron is cut into the outer edge (a notch);
    on light weights the whole stroke bends into the chevron so it never thins or breaks,
    as in Unbounded's own light B and E.
  - Accented letters built from these glyphs inherit the chevron.

OTHER CHANGES
  - Family renamed to "Dutch Display Sans" in all name records.
  - Static instances for weights 200-900; no variable font in 2.000.
  - Generated with build_dds2.py (included).
